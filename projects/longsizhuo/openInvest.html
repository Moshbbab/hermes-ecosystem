overview

OpenInvest is a self-hosted investment decision engine designed to function as a plugin for AI agents. It provides a multi-agent investment committee with features for evidence-based reasoning, long-horizon backtesting, and auditable decision records.

  • Multi-agent coordinator-worker architecture
  • Provides an auditable decision trail
  • Includes a long-horizon backtesting harness
